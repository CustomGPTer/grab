Release notes

What changed in each version of Grab. The newest is at the top.

Version 2.2 21 November 2026Latest

  • Adverts on video sites stopped earlier. Adverts on video sites are stopped before they are lined up: the advert instructions are taken out of the page before the site's player reads them.
  • A fixed hold that some players apply before a video starts is cut short.
  • An advert that starts anyway is covered with “Skipping advert…”, muted and skipped at once. If it is still stuck after 8 seconds, the cover lifts.
  • The grey play-button picture shown before a video loads is gone, on every site.
  • A setting, on by default, that stops these adverts loading.
  • An advert timing section in the page report (menu › Copy page report).
  • Grab's code for this version is published under GPL-3.0 as Grab-2.2-source.zip on the release.

Version 2.1 14 November 2026

  • Sizes on a grid, and renaming. The size sheet is a grid of tiles, one for each resolution the site offers, with sound only as M4A or MP3 alongside. Each tile shows its file size where the site states one.
  • Rename, on the size sheet, to choose the file's name before it downloads.
  • Rename in the Files tab, for files you have already saved.
  • Pick a tile, then tap Download. The size you chose last time is already picked for you; the first time, it is the highest.

Version 2.0 13 November 2026

  • A new Download button, a home page and Settings. A round Download button that is grey until Grab finds a video on the page, then turns red, with a pulse and a count of what it found. It still works while grey, and you can drag it anywhere on the page.
  • A home page with a search box, shortcut tiles for the sites you use most and the pages you visited recently. History is in the browser menu.
  • Private mode: separate logins, no history, and everything wiped when you leave it.
  • Save privately keeps a file inside Grab, out of your gallery. The Files tab shows Gallery and Private separately and can move a file between them.
  • A built-in player with speed and subtitle controls, swipe to seek and double-tap to jump ten seconds. It carries on where you stopped.
  • Subtitles saved inside the video when the site has them, and a cover picture with title and artist on sound files.
  • Just a section: give a start and an end time to download part of a video.
  • Start later: set a time for a download to begin.
  • Pause and resume, for one download or for all of them.
  • A Wi-Fi only switch, off until you turn it on.
  • When you open Grab after copying a link in another app, it offers to download it.
  • A Settings tab for all of the above, with the theme, the start page, the button's colour, and buttons to clear history and browser data.
  • Up to three downloads can run at once. Two is still the starting setting.

Version 1.4

  • Adverts in video players. Adverts that play inside a site's video player are skipped. The blocklist cannot stop these, because they are sent from the same servers as the video itself.

Version 1.3

  • Pages that would not respond. On some sites a pop-up box, such as a cookie notice, was squashed to a sliver, leaving the page dimmed and impossible to scroll or tap. Pages are now laid out at the full size of the screen.

Version 1.2

  • Page tools. Remove page overlay, in the browser menu, clears a box or a dimmed layer that is stuck over a page.
  • Copy page report, in the browser menu, gathers the details of a page that misbehaves so the problem is easy to report.

Version 1.1

  • A thorough overhaul. If the download engine does not recognise a site, Grab offers the stream the page itself was playing.
  • A link with several videos gives a tick list, and each file is named after its own video.
  • The blocklist now covers about 77,000 advertising and tracking servers. Pop-ups and redirects are stopped, with a prompt to open one if you want it, and a shield in the address bar switches blocking off for one site.
  • The download engine fetches its newest version once a day.
  • The queue is saved, so downloads survive the app being closed, and a retry carries on from the part already downloaded.
  • A button to clear the browser's cookies, logins and cache.
  • Every size the site offers is listed, and video is always saved as MP4.
  • Two downloads run at once.
  • A download is tried without your login first. Only if that fails does Grab retry with its browser's cookies for that one site.
  • The Download button could send the address of the page you opened first, not the one you had moved on to.
  • A page whose video sits inside a one-item list failed with an error.
  • More than twenty smaller faults found in a review of version 1.0.

Version 1.0

  • A built-in browser with a Download button for the page you are on.
  • Share a link to Grab from any other app, or paste one in.
  • Video or sound only, with a choice of quality.
  • Downloads carry on in the background, with progress in a notification.
  • The yt-dlp download engine and FFmpeg, inside the app.

Checking your download

The file for version 2.2 is called Grab.apk. It is 81.9 MB (81,882,036 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

ed078d197c7a57f382637bcd9104440295d1a916ee23c6ce318c47df27322f98

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile Grab.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.

If Google Play Protect blocks it

Why it happens. Google Play Protect checks apps installed from outside Google Play. Sites change how they serve videos all the time, so Grab fetches the newest version of its download engine once a day. Play Protect is wary of apps that download new code after they are installed, because harmful apps do that too, so it can block Grab whatever the code actually does. It may say "Harmful app blocked" or "This app may be harmful".

What Grab actually does. It is a web browser that saves videos and sound from the pages you open. It has no adverts, no account and no servers of its own, so the developer never sees what you browse or save. Its code is free software, published for every version.

Check your file first. Make sure the SHA-256 checksum of your download matches the one above, so you know it is the genuine file from this page.

To install it. Open the Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Play Protect, then Settings (the cog). Turn off Scan apps with Play Protect. Install Grab, then turn the setting straight back on.

Afterwards. Play Protect may still warn about Grab in a later scan. Updates install over the old version in the same way and keep everything you have saved.
