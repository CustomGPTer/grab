Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026. This policy covers the Grab app for Android (package uk.jj.grab) and this website. Grab is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom.

The short version

  • Grab has no accounts and no servers of its own. The developer never receives your browsing, your downloads or anything else from your phone.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking in the app.
  • Your history, shortcuts, logins, download queue and downloaded files are stored on your phone and nowhere else.

What stays on your phone

The pages you have visited, your home-page shortcuts, the cookies and logins of the sites you use in Grab's browser, your download queue and its history, your settings, and the place you stopped in each video you play are kept in the app's private storage on your device.

Downloaded files are saved to the Movies/Grab and Music/Grab folders on your phone or, if you choose Save privately, inside the app's own storage. Grab does not ask for access to your photos, your other files, your contacts, your location, your camera or your microphone.

Copied links

When you open Grab, it checks whether the last thing you copied is a web link, so that it can offer to download it. The check is made on your phone. Nothing you copied is sent anywhere unless you choose to download that link, and a switch in Settings turns the check off.

Private mode

Private mode uses a separate set of cookies and logins and keeps no history. All of it is erased when you leave private mode.

The websites you visit

Grab is a web browser and a downloader. When you open a page or download a video, your phone connects directly to that website, as it would from any other browser. Nothing passes through the developer.

Those websites receive your device's internet address, as they do from any browser, and handle what they receive under their own privacy policies.

Grab blocks requests to about 77,000 known advertising and tracking servers, and stops pop-ups. The shield in the address bar switches this off for the site you are on, and a switch in Settings turns it off altogether.

GitHub receives your device's internet address, as any website does, and handles it under its own privacy policy.

Web pages are shown using Android System WebView, the web component that Google builds into Android. As in every app that uses it, it can check pages against Google's Safe Browsing list of dangerous sites and, if you have agreed on your phone to share usage and diagnostics with Google, send Google diagnostic data. Grab adds nothing to either.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Data on your phone is kept until you remove it. Settings has a button to clear your history and another to clear the browser's cookies, logins and cache.
  • Downloaded files stay until you delete them, from the Files tab in Grab or from your gallery or music app.
  • Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Grab deletes everything it holds, including files you saved privately. Files in Movies/Grab and Music/Grab stay on your phone.

Your rights

UK and EU data protection law gives you rights over personal data that an organisation holds about you, including the right to see it, correct it and have it deleted.

The developer of Grab holds no personal data about you: there is no account, no server and no log of who uses the app. Your data is on your own phone, where you control it directly using the steps above. If you need help doing that, use the contact link below. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Security

The app's data is held in private storage that other apps cannot read, and is left out of Android's backups. When login cookies are needed for a download, they are passed to the download engine in a temporary file that is deleted as soon as the engine has finished.

Grab connects over HTTPS wherever a website offers it. A few older websites offer only an unencrypted connection, and Grab, like other browsers, will still open them.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other websites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub, as the host, receives the internet address of visitors and handles it under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The app file is downloaded from GitHub as well, on the same terms.

Children

Grab is a general-purpose browser and downloader. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the date at the top changed.
